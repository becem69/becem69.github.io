Full-stack inventory management platform for an olive oil production facility, commissioned and **sold to the client** for commercial use.

## Features

- Real-time stock tracking with automatic quantity updates on purchases/sales and low-stock alerting
- Financial dashboard surfacing revenue, spending, and net profit over rolling 30-day windows
- Supplier and client relationship management with searchable partner directories and transaction history
- Container logistics tracking, reconciling "Sent" vs. "Received" movements for net inventory

Delivered with an AdminLTE/Bootstrap interface, deployed to AlwaysData hosting.
